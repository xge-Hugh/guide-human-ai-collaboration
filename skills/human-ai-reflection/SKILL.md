# Human-AI Reflection

Reflection is a joint evidence-building activity, not an AI completion summary or an action-item quota.

## Runtime behavior

1. **Restore the actual reflection scope.** Use explicit commitments, observed friction, failures, recoveries, and participant questions. Do not invent a checklist merely because the task ended.

2. **Separate evaluation objects.** At minimum distinguish when relevant:
   - task outcome and evidence;
   - human judgment/contribution and its support conditions;
   - AI first-opportunity behavior and recovered behavior;
   - coordination or assurance failures;
   - human compensatory labor used to repair carrier/AI misses;
   - workflow or tool constraints.

3. **Treat causal explanations as hypotheses until supported.** Use concrete episodes, counterfactuals, evidence, or competing explanations. A coherent story is not proof of why something happened.

4. **Do not manufacture action items.** “No action now” is valid when evidence does not justify change. Record a re-trigger condition when that is more useful than a speculative fix.

5. **Do not confuse task success with capability growth.** A successful collaborative result does not prove independent human mastery, transfer, long-term growth, or reliable carrier behavior.

6. **Preserve recovered failures.** If a key behavior occurred only after the human corrected or reminded the AI, record it as recovery rather than rewriting the episode as a clean pass.

7. **Close agreed reflection deliberately.** If the collaboration explicitly included a retrospective, do not silently replace it with an AI summary. The human may explicitly skip or narrow it. Do not block unrelated delivery when reflection was never part of the agreement.

## Output discipline

Prefer a small number of evidence-bearing findings over exhaustive scoring. Keep unresolved explanations marked as hypotheses or unknowns. Route new research questions or implementation debt to their proper owner instead of forcing every observation into an immediate process change.
