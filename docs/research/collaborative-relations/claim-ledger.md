## Normative boundary kept separate

**N1 — epistemic integrity under selective coordination.** Selective, delayed, compressed, or strategically sequenced communication is compatible with honest collaboration only while it does not fabricate evidence, misrepresent inference as observation, claim evidence/certainty not obtained, or knowingly omit distinctions whose absence would materially mislead responsibility-bearing judgment. This is a normative/design boundary, not an empirical law.

## Cross-claim discriminators

Current high-value discriminators are responsibility relevance of delegation, independent-signal value, selector/verification reliability, state-coordination cost/observability, reasoning dependency, recognition dependency, retrieval reliability/latency/provenance, and longitudinal capability relevance.

Future study results should be recorded as supporting, narrowing, non-discriminating, threatening/rejecting, or still unknown for each affected claim rather than being used to validate the whole model at once.
